## S-62: endmembers and overlay

- **Endmembers:** VCA. On synthetic mixtures of splib07 spectra (SNR 30 dB, 20 seeds, m = 5, 8 and 10) its mean spectral angle to truth matched N-FINDR's within 0.1°, and it is faster: 5.8 s against 7.5 s on a 1 M-pixel, 87-band Cuprite crop, MNF included. PPI + VCA cost 5,629 B gzip, under the 12 KB rule, so `spectral` takes them.
- **Overlay:** `polygon-clipping` 0.15.7 intersects 50k parcels with 50k parcels (200k candidate pairs) in 10.8 / 12.1 s, with no exception in about 336k clips. Overlay stays in JS. Its one failure is a limit of 1,000,000 sweep events per call ("queue size too big"), which a single-call dissolve or union of a whole large layer reaches, so #56 builds unions and dissolves in parts.

## S-63: hydrology at 48 M posts

- Fill, D8, accumulation, Strahler order and watersheds peak at 284 MiB (6.2 B/post) on a 1 m 3DEP DEM, and at 467 MiB with D-infinity. The worst input found reached 748 MiB.
- The hard limit under the 1 GiB cap is 81.6 M posts with the fill queue sized once (a growing `Vec` lowers it to about 64 M), so the 48 M refusal stands, with no GPU and no tiling.
- The chain takes 26–39 s at 48 M posts, 85 % of it in the fill, so the op reports progress per stage and can be cancelled between stages.

## S-64: EGM2008 1′

- Source: GeographicLib `egm2008-1.tar.bz2`, 162,388,303 B, SHA-256 `bdb382d0be7ece9142450eacc24b7b7f0889ee3e0ba4f535b04ec383f94c0fb5`. The grid is 21,600 × 10,801.
- Use 5° tiles: cubic interpolation needs one sample before and two after each cell, so a tile is 303 × 303. Each tile is stored with the EGM96 grid's planar predictor and deflate, giving a 76.7 MB pack (stored would be 476 MB).
- A cold tile takes 3–4 ms from a `File` or an OPFS blob. OPFS is unavailable over `file://`.
- `geod` inflates the tiles and keeps an LRU of 32 tiles (5.7 MiB). Jobs are batched by tile, because scattered global points miss any cache.
- At 1,000 points, including tile edges, the poles and ±180°, tiled results equal the full grid. The `GeoidEval` comparison waits for #59.
- Avoid `f64::clamp` in `geod`: it pulls in float formatting and breaks the budget.

## S-65: `aviation`

- `opt: 's'` saves 1,207 B (2.6 %) and runs about 10 % slower on 4.9 M points from the DFC2018 Houston LAS, so the bundle stays at opt 3.
- A table-driven Amendment 18 prototype, covering Tables 4-1 to 4-15, adds 6,362 B at opt 3. With what it leaves out, expect about 54–56 KB, which fits the 64 KiB budget and needs no separate `aviation-a18` bundle.
