# GIS Workbench 2.0 — validation report (release R4)

This report covers the 2.0 Modernization Plan (`Docs/SDP-2.0-modernization-plan.md`), phases P0–P8, which were released as 2.0.0-beta.1 through rc.2 and then 2.0.0 on 2026-10-28. Design records are DN-53 to DN-59 in `docs/design/decisions.md`. Requirements are the R4 rows of Appendix A (SHL, NET, GLB, CON, ING, CNV, LAI, PIP, HUB).

## Not verified by the build

These need people or third-party applications:
- Opening the PSD in Photoshop, the PDF in Illustrator, the GLB in Blender, the USDZ in Quick Look, and the data package in ATAK or WinTAK. The files are parsed back by independent readers in the tests.
- The Apps Script relay against a real deployment (S-41).
- The usability study and Adam Simmons's review of the release candidates.
