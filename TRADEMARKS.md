# GIS Workbench trademark policy

GIS Workbench is open source. The code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0, which lets you
use, change and share it freely. The name and logo are different: they say where the software
comes from. This policy explains how you may use them, so that people can always tell the
official project apart from forks and from products that only work with it.

## Summary

- **You may** use the name to talk about the project, to say your work runs with or is based on
  it, and to share unchanged official releases.
- **You may not** use the name or logo for a modified version, for your own product or service, or
  in a way that suggests the project endorses you, without written permission.
- **Forks are welcome.** Give yours a different name and say it is "based on GIS Workbench".

names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor", except to describe where
the work came from and to reproduce the NOTICE file. This policy is where any permission to use

## Uses that need no permission

Make sure each of these is accurate and not misleading:

1. **Referring to the project.** For example, in articles, books, talks, courses, videos,
   reviews, comparisons, bug reports and academic papers.
2. **Describing compatibility.** For example, "works with GIS Workbench", "exports recipes for
   GIS Workbench" or "a plugin for GIS Workbench". Your own product name must come first and be
   more prominent, and it must not include "GIS Workbench".
3. **Saying where your work comes from.** For example, "based on GIS Workbench" or "a fork of
   GIS Workbench", in your description, README or About box.
4. **Sharing official releases unchanged.** For example, copying, mirroring or bundling the
   release files under their own name, with the published `SHA256SUMS`. Packaging them for a Linux
   distribution or an internal catalogue counts, as long as the files are byte-for-byte identical.
5. **Community activity.** For example, user groups, meetups, workshops, chat channels and
   social-media accounts about the project. Their names must make clear they are unofficial,
   such as "GIS Workbench users (unofficial)". They must not charge for membership in a way that
   suggests official status.
6. **Internal use.** For example, running and naming the software inside your organisation,
   including modified builds that never leave it.

## Uses that need written permission

Ask first (see [Contact](#contact)) before you:

1. **Ship a modified version under the Marks.** "Modified" means any build that is not
   byte-for-byte an official release. That includes changed code, a changed build configuration,
   added or removed features, and different bundled data or AI packs.
2. **Use the Marks in a product, service, company or project name.** For example, "GIS Workbench
   Pro", "GIS Workbench Cloud" or "Acme GIS Workbench".
3. **Use the Marks in a domain name, app-store listing or package name** that people could
   mistake for an official one.
4. **Offer a hosted service** that presents itself as GIS Workbench.
5. **Make merchandise** that carries the Marks, such as clothing, stickers or printed media.
6. **Suggest endorsement or partnership.** For example, "official", "certified", "approved" or
   "recommended by the GIS Workbench project".

## Uses that are never allowed

- Names, logos or domains that are **confusingly similar** to the Marks. That includes
  misspellings, prefixes, suffixes and translations used to trade on the project's name.
- **Registering the Marks**, or anything confusingly similar, as your own trademark, company name,
  domain name or social-media handle.
- Using the Marks in a way that is **false, misleading or harmful**. Examples are distributing
  malware or tampered builds under the name, or presenting a modified build as an official release.

## Forks and modified builds

To distribute a modified version:

1. Choose a name and icon that cannot be confused with GIS Workbench.
2. Change every place the app shows its name: the window and page title, the About box, splash
   and Help text, the file names of the built HTML, and the user agent or identifier the app sends
   to services.
3. Keep the copyright and licence notices that the Apache License requires.
4. You may say "based on GIS Workbench". Please link to the official repository.
5. Do not publish your build with the official `SHA256SUMS`, and do not describe it as a release
   of GIS Workbench.

## How to show the Marks

- Write the name as **GIS Workbench**: two words, capital G-I-S and W. Don't use "GISWorkbench",

- Use the name as an adjective where you can, for example "the GIS Workbench app".
- Do not change the logo or icon, except to resize them in proportion.
- Where it fits, add: "GIS Workbench is a trademark of D2Insights LLC."
